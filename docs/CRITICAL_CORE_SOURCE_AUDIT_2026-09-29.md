## Counting and interpretation

There are 80 currency/factor combinations, of which 60 are critical. A raw input is **not** a certified factor: Yields needs both maturities and the complete eight-currency cross-section; Inflation needs comparable CPI and policy inputs across all eight currencies. Filling one missing maturity does not earn a whole critical coverage point.

Risk is structurally partial: the existing formula retains 50% of the stable baseline anchor, plus current-account/debt relative components. Commodity has no defined raw-basket-to-normalized-factor transformation. These are model-definition constraints, not proof that a paid source is necessary. Both are deliberately preserved, not silently redesigned.

The existing display rules remain: FULL LIVE at 80/80; LIVE at 60/60 critical with no wholly absent Core category; PARTIAL LIVE if critical/category gaps remain; BASELINE without sufficient observed inputs. Research availability and ML training status are not Core source coverage. The previous strict forecast calibration remains separate from the display label.
